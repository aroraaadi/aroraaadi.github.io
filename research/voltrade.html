SPY vol strategy

A standalone book on the options sleeve that sells SPY premium when implied vol exceeds the vol the market tends to realise, and stays out when the regime, the curve or the calendar says not to. The rules come from the Nelson notes on IV rank, the Greeks and roll windows; each one was tested in a synthetic backtest before it was allowed to trade, and the ones that failed are still shown. Suggest-only; the sleeve is not funded.
