Vol engine

Realised, forecast and implied volatility of SPY, measured the way a vol desk measures them: five range-based estimators; seven forecasters raced out of sample since 2010 on the losses that survive a noisy target, with Diebold-Mariano tests between them; SVI smiles fitted per expiry and checked for butterfly and calendar arbitrage, with the risk-neutral density each one implies; model-free implied variance the VIX way; and how rough and how mean-reverting vol is. The forecaster that wins the race is the one the SPY vol strategy compares implied against.

Smile fit, SVI

Raw SVI (Gatheral 2004) in log-forward-moneyness, fitted per expiry. Butterfly: the Gatheral-Jacquier g-function must stay non-negative. Calendar: total variance must not fall with maturity. The density is Breeden-Litzenberger's second derivative of the fitted call price; its skew and kurtosis are what the market prices, model-free of the underlying.
