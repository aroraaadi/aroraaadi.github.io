Vol surface

The SPY implied-volatility surface as the market quotes it: every usable strike and expiry, Black-Scholes implied from the bid-ask mid with a dividend yield, drawn as a surface, as smiles and as a term structure, and priced three ways. A browser left open on the machine that runs python3 -m options.live --watch 5 follows the market; the published page shows the last snapshot pushed.

Which model fits the smile

Goh, Korovedzai, Chen and Bhattacharyya (SSRN 7192821) ask which pricing model fits S&P options best and answer on one date with placeholder parameters. This is the same question asked of today's chain the other way round: each model is calibrated to the even strikes and scored on the odd ones, in vol points, so the number is a held-out fit, not a fit to the data it was shown. Black-Scholes at each quote's own implied vol is the data, not a model, and is not scored. A better fit today says nothing about tomorrow's prices.
