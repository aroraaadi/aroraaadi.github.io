What this screen cannot do

Two inputs the brief asked for are not here, and the reason is stated rather than filled with a stand-in:

    The one thing the screen refuses to do is sort by the pre-run profile. That profile is computed and flagged for every name — it is what was asked for — but in 266,460 company-months of point-in-time small-cap history it underperformed the universe by 3.4 points a year and doubled least often of any bucket, and the repo's own Fama-MacBeth test puts small-cap price momentum at t = −4. A screen that ranked on it would be a machine for finding the names that did worst.

    Personal research project, not investment advice. A base rate is what happened to names like this one, not what will happen to it; no signal here is established at |t| ≥ 3.
